Three steps

Built for the moment the cart is ready.

  1. Open the cart

    Use CartQuote from the Chrome tab where you are already building a retailer cart.

  2. Review the summary

    Check the extracted descriptions, quantities and item prices. Edit anything that needs correction.

  3. Save the record

    Download a dated PDF price summary when you are satisfied with the details.

Useful when

Personal records
