Capture allowance and downloads

What is an AI cart capture?

It is one request for CartQuote to read a shopping cart and prepare a reviewed item-price summary. A successful capture counts even if you do not download a PDF. Known upstream failures are refunded; an interrupted request whose outcome cannot be confirmed may still count.

How do Pro allowances work?

Pro monthly and Pro annual each include 100 AI cart captures per UTC calendar month. The allowance is shared across devices using the same licence and unused captures do not roll over to the next month. The Free plan also resets each UTC calendar month. Pro replaces its five-capture allowance; it is not unlimited AI use.

Do PDF downloads use a capture?

No. Downloading or re-downloading a PDF already saved in local Quote History does not use another AI capture. Those saved PDFs can be downloaded without a separate download limit. Quote History currently keeps up to 20 PDFs, and may keep fewer if browser storage is full; it is not an indefinite archive.

Are there overage charges?

No. CartQuote does not bill overage charges. If an allowance is unavailable, no new AI capture is started until the next UTC calendar month. Temporary service-capacity limits can also pause new captures.

What the PDF covers: item prices shown in the cart and reviewed by you. It does not represent shipping, order-level discounts, additional tax, an official retailer quote or invoice, or proof of purchase or ownership. Capacity: there are no overage charges; temporary service-capacity limits may pause new AI captures.
